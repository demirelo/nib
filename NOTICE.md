## Browser word list

`Browser/words.js` has two parts.

1. Words from Webster's Second International Dictionary (published in 1934), through the BSD `web2` word list. FreeBSD and macOS distribute this list as `/usr/share/dict/web2`. The file `/usr/share/dict/README` on macOS says:

   > Welcome to web2 (Webster's Second International) all 234,936 words worth.
   > The 1934 copyright has lapsed, according to the supplier.

   The list is in the public domain in the United States, because the copyright of the 1934 edition expired.

2. Additional modern vocabulary, written for this project. The MIT License of Nib covers it.
